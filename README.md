# Neurise

**Neurise is an SEO, AI Search Visibility and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) agency based in Poland.**

We help brands improve their visibility across traditional search and AI-powered discovery platforms, including **Google, ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity**.

## What we do

- Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
- AI Search Visibility
- Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
- Entity Optimization
- Technical SEO
- Content Strategy
- AI Visibility Monitoring

## About Neurise

Neurise combines SEO with optimization for generative search and large language models. We help brands become easier to discover, understand and recommend across both traditional search engines and AI systems.
